About Meal Mithra

Meal Mithra is a food-sharing platform designed to connect food donors with people who need meals. Restaurants, households, events and individuals can share surplus food through the platform.


Our goal is to reduce food wastage and encourage people to share food with their community. Every meal shared can make a small difference.
